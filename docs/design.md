# KWIS design direction

Reference inspection: https://exarth.com/ (27 October 2026). Initially blocked by the runtime policy; reachable after network configuration changed. Its home page uses capability groups, selected projects, process checkpoints, a technology toolkit, industry groupings, client stories, and a focused contact invitation. Internal About and Services routes were also reviewed through their accessible markup. No images, fonts, logo, source code, testimonials, statistics, or copy were taken from it.

KWIS uses an original orbit-and-monogram illustration, midnight navy surfaces, mint and periwinkle accents, generous editorial headings, restrained card borders, and simple directional controls. The public site prioritizes a clear proposition, capabilities, company introduction, process, genuine work, tools, and contact. The dashboard uses the same visual language with denser layouts and separate draft/publish actions.

Business facts supplied: company name (including the supplied spelling “Knowlege”), full name, and city/province/country. Service and technology seed content is explicitly illustrative. No invented people, clients, projects, ratings, experience totals, street addresses, or coordinates are seeded. Legal text is marked as draft pending review.

Accessibility: semantic headings and forms, explicit labels, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, mobile navigation, decorative illustrations hidden from assistive technology, keyboard reorder buttons alongside dragging, and safe rendered rich text. Browser tests cover desktop routes, a 375px phone viewport, menu interactions, and overflow. No Lighthouse scores or formal WCAG certification are claimed.
